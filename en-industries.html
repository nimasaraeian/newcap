Industries we serve

One cap. Many industries.

A closure is judged by what sits underneath it. Each sector we supply asks something different of the same small part — and that is what shapes the design.

Detergents

Household, laundry and cleaning

Thick, surfactant-heavy liquids that creep, foam and attack weak materials. The closure has to open easily in a wet hand and still seal after twenty cycles.

  • Surfactant resistance
  • Measuring caps
  • Wet-grip ribbing
  • Tamper evidence
Oil & lubricants

Engine oil, brake fluid and lubricants

Oils creep through any gap, and brake fluid attacks weak materials and draws moisture from the air. The closure has to seal tightly and consistently, because a leaking bottle on a shelf or in a workshop is both a loss and a hazard.

  • Leak-free sealing
  • Brake-fluid caps
  • Moisture protection
  • Custom colours
Chemicals & adhesives

Industrial chemicals, glues and adhesives

Aggressive contents, and products that cure on contact with air. The closure has to resist the chemistry, keep air out so an adhesive does not harden in its container, and stay safe wherever children could reach it.

  • Chemical resistance
  • Airtight sealing
  • Child-resistant caps
  • Adhesive container bases
Food & packaging

Food products and industrial packaging

From food containers to 20-litre gallons, the closure has to be hygienic, consistent from one batch to the next, and show clearly whether the pack has been opened.

  • Hygienic design
  • Gallon caps
  • Tamper-evident bands
  • Batch consistency
